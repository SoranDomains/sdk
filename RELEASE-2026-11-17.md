# Individual name sales in XLM

Holder 0.12.0 adds fixed-price sales for eligible individual names. Sellers can quote, list and cancel. Buyers can review the signed price and fee split before purchasing in XLM. Owner 0.13.1 recognizes the marketplace Registrar when checking governed namespace upgrades. Both releases require Node.js 22.12 or later and `@stellar/stellar-sdk >=17.4.0 <18`. [Holder sale examples](packages/holder/README.md#individual-name-sales-in-xlm) · [Owner upgrade guide](packages/owner/README.md)
