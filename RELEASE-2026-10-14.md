# October 14 signing fixes

The prepared patch releases are **Holder 0.10.1** and **Owner 0.12.1**. npm
publication is pending. Lookup remains 0.11.0 and needs no new release.

Both packages validate that simulated legacy account-signed writes still match
the requested transaction source, operation, contract, method and arguments.
They reject unexpected authorization identities, extra authorization roots and
nested calls before asking the wallet to sign. Permissionless upkeep accepts no
authorization. Holder also uses the configured contract-wallet holder identity
for network-address writes and preserves permissionless upkeep through its fee
payer without requesting contract-wallet authorization.

test adaptations are recorded in `sync-manifest.json` and `test-manifest.json`.
